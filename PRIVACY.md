# Privacy Policy — BYOK Store

_Last updated: 2026-09-19_

**BYOK Store** is a bring‑your‑own‑key (BYOK) app. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own.

## What the app stores
All your data (entries, settings and your API key) is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## What leaves your device
BYOK Store fetches a public catalog file from GitHub Pages and downloads APKs from GitHub Releases when you tap Install. No AI requests are made by the store itself. It reads which catalog apps are installed on your device only to show Open/Install buttons; nothing is uploaded.

## Permissions
- **Internet** — required only to reach the AI provider you chose.
- **Install unknown apps** — asked once by Android the first time you install an APK from the store.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
